# Introduction to PVP

## Gearing Up for PVP

Gearing up for PVP in World of Warcraft is essential to ensure you have the best chance of success in battlegrounds and arenas. This guide will cover the different types of gear available, how to obtain them, and the best practices for gearing up your character. It is also listed in logical order of aqquisition.

### Bloody Tokens

Bloody Tokens are a currency that can be earned by completing world quests while in the War Mode. They can be used to purchase PVP gear 3 ilvl lower than the current season's PVP gear. This is a great way to get started with PVP gear.

Typically, you will want to purchase the following items with Bloody Tokens. These are typically the cheapest item to purchase with Bloody Tokens and will give you a good boost to your stats.

- **Cloak**
- **Bracers**
- **Belt**

As the season progresses, you will easily acquire enough Bloody Tokens to purchase more items. You will want to purchase all possible items with Bloody Tokens as they are the cheapest item to purchase. They can eventually be replaced with Conquest or crafted gear.

### Crafting Gear

Crafting gear isn't only a great way to get started with PVP gear, it is also essential because it is the only way to get embellishments. You will get 9 crafting tokens from your first PVP win. Necks and Rings costs 3 tokens, while other items cost 4 tokens. Very quickly, you should be able to craft 3 pieces of gear with your first tokens. You can buy more tokens from one of the pvp vendors.

Typically, you will want to craft the following items with Embellishments. These are typically the best items to craft with Embellishments and will give you a good boost to your stats.

- **Neck**: 3 tokens
- **Ring**: 3 tokens
- 1 of the following (depending on your class and spec and what stats you need):
  - **Boots**: 4 tokens
  - **Belt**: 4 tokens
- 1 of the following (or more if your class doesn't use the bonus from the tier set)
  - **Chest**
  - **Shoulders**
  - **Helm**
  - **Pants**
  - **Gloves**

#### Embellishments

You can have 2 embellishments total. The best embellishments are based on your class and spec. Not all embilishments will fit on all gear, so be sure to check that before crafting your items.

### Honor Gear

Fill in the rest of the gear slots with Honor Gear. You can purchase Honor Gear with Honor Points, which are earned by completing any PVP activity.

Typically, you will want to purchase the following items with Honor Gear:

- **Trinkets**

### Conquest Gear

Conquest Gear is the best PVP gear available (high versatility, same ilvl as crafted gear). You can purchase Conquest Gear with Conquest Points, which are earned by completing rated battlegrounds and arenas or by looting warcrate in the world.

Typically, you will want to purchase the following items first with Conquest Gear, they are the items that will be transformed into tier gear:

- **Helm**
- **Chest**
- **Shoulders**
- **Pants**
- **Gloves**

### Final Notes

Once you get more conquest points and have enough to craft or buy the rest of the conquest gear, you will want to replace the lower ilvl gear with the higher ilvl gear, as it is the best PVP gear available.

## Interface and Addons

### Mandatory Addons

- **BattlegroundEnemies**: It shows you the enemy team and their health and makes them targetable. It also shows you the enemy team's cooldowns and buffs.
- **BigDebuffs**: It shows you the enemy team's cooldowns and buffs directly on the nameplates and unit frames.
